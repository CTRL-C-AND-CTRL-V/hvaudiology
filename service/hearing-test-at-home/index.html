Photo: home visit with portable test equipment

If getting to a clinic is difficult, we can come to you. Our audiologist brings portable diagnostic equipment and completes a patient-centred hearing test in your home.

Who home visits suit

  • People with limited mobility or who no longer drive
  • Residents of aged care homes and retirement villages
  • People recovering from illness or surgery
  • Families who want to be there to support a parent

What happens on the day

A home visit follows the same steps as an assessment in the clinic. We need a quiet room and somewhere to sit at a table. Before we leave, we explain your results and agree on the next steps with you.

Check we cover your area

Home visits depend on where you live. Call us on 0448 187 960 and we'll let you know.
