Photo: custom earplugs in a range of colours

Hearing damage from noise is permanent, but it can be prevented. We supply earplugs that bring noise down to a safer level while keeping speech and music clear.

Earplugs for every situation

  • Music and events. Filtered plugs lower the volume evenly, so music still sounds natural.
  • Work and construction. Protection for noisy sites, workshops and machinery.
  • Sleep and travel. Comfortable plugs for a snoring partner, a noisy street or a long flight.

Custom-fitted earplugs

Custom earplugs are made from an impression of your ears, so they seal well and stay comfortable for hours. We take the impression in the clinic.
