A hearing assessment shows how well each ear hears across the sounds of everyday speech, and why. We use current diagnostic equipment and take the time to explain what the results mean for you.

What the assessment covers

  • A conversation about your hearing, your health history and the situations you find hardest
  • A look inside your ear canals to check for wax or anything else that could affect the results
  • Tests of how well you hear tones and speech in each ear
  • Middle-ear tests where they are needed

After the assessment

We go through your results with you on the day. If you have a hearing loss, we talk through your options, which may include hearing aids, communication strategies or a referral to your GP or an ENT specialist.

Who should book

Book an assessment if you often ask people to repeat themselves, need the TV louder than others do, or struggle to follow conversations in noisy places. It is also worth booking if you have ringing in your ears. A baseline test is useful from middle age onwards, even if you haven't noticed any problems.
