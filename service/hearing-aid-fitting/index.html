Choosing a hearing aid is only the start. We help you pick the right device for your hearing and lifestyle, fit it precisely, and fine-tune it over follow-up visits until it feels natural.

Independent advice

As an independent clinic, we are not tied to one manufacturer. We fit devices from brands including Widex, Phonak, Unitron and Signia, and recommend the one that suits you.

How fitting works

  1. Choosing. We talk about where you struggle to hear, your budget, and the style you prefer: invisible, behind-the-ear or receiver-in-canal.
  2. Fitting. We program the devices to your hearing results and check they sit comfortably.
  3. Follow-up. We adjust the settings as you get used to them. Some fine-tuning over the first few weeks is normal.

Ongoing care

We clean and check your hearing aids, handle repairs, and update settings if your hearing changes. Read our guide to hearing aids for tips on looking after them.
