Photo: microsuction procedure under the microscope

We remove ear wax by microsuction, a gentle, dry method performed under magnification. Our audiologists are trained in the technique.

What is microsuction?

We examine your ear canal under a microscope, then use a low-pressure suction device to lift the wax out. Because we can see the ear canal throughout, the procedure is controlled and precise. An appointment takes about 30 minutes.

Why we don't use water

  • Clear visibility. The whole procedure is monitored under the microscope.
  • Mess-free. No liquid goes in the ear, so the risk of infection is lower than with syringing or irrigation.
  • Less unsteadiness. Without water in the ear, you're less likely to feel dizzy afterwards.
  • Suitable for more ears. It can be used with a perforated eardrum, a mastoid cavity or a foreign object in the ear canal.

Before your appointment

Use ear drops or an ear spray for 3 to 4 days beforehand, unless you have recently had ear surgery or an ear infection. Without softening, the wax may not come out, and the appointment fee still applies.

When we can't remove wax

We don't perform microsuction if you have an ear infection or ear pain. Please see your GP first. If you have a cold or the flu, wait until you've recovered before booking. For urgent discomfort, call us directly on 0448 187 960.
