Ear infections

Common symptoms, when to see a doctor, and checking your hearing afterwards.

Hearing health

  • Anatomy of the ear

Photo: otoscope examination of the ear

Middle-ear infections are most common in children, but they can happen at any age. Most clear up with medical treatment, but they can affect your hearing while they last.

Symptoms of ear infections

  • Ear pain
  • A feeling of fullness or pressure in the ear
  • Difficulty hearing
  • Fever
  • Headaches
  • Dizziness or problems with balance
  • Fluid draining from the ear
  • In children: irritability, trouble sleeping or tugging at the ear

See your GP

Ear infections need medical assessment and treatment. If you have ear pain, a fever or discharge from the ear, see your doctor first.

How we can help

Once your doctor has cleared the infection, we can test your hearing to check it has returned to normal and look into any ongoing problems. We work alongside GPs and ENT specialists, and can refer you on if needed.
