Services

Hearing services

Nine services across hearing tests, hearing aids, tinnitus and ear care, at our clinics in Rutherford and Muswellbrook.

Hearing tests

Diagnostic assessments in the clinic, at home, or for work and government programs.

  • Hearing Assessment for AdultsA full diagnostic hearing assessment, explained in plain language.
  • Hearing Test at HomeA full hearing test in the comfort of your own home.
  • Hearing Assessment for VeteransAssessments and hearing aids through the Hearing Services Program and DVA.
  • Hearing Assessment for OccupationPre-employment testing for NSW Police, commercial driving and industrial roles.

Hearing aids and tinnitus

Choosing, fitting and fine-tuning hearing aids, and relief for ringing in the ears.

  • Hearing Aid FittingPrecise fitting and adjustment for comfort and clarity.
  • Hearing Aids for TinnitusHearing aids with built-in sound therapy to ease tinnitus.
  • Tinnitus ManagementTinnitus assessment and management with sound therapy and support.

Ear care

Wax removal by microsuction, and earplugs to protect the hearing you have.

  • Ear Wax RemovalSafe, professional wax removal by microsuction.
  • Ear PlugsEarplugs for music, noisy work, sleep and travel.
